# Contributions and AI policy

Oxigraph is a volunteer-maintained project.
As such, the bandwidth of the maintainers is sadly quite limited.

Please keep your issues and pull requests short and on-point to help us provide speedy reviews.
For significant contributions, please open an issue to discuss the goal and design before starting implementation.
It's better to have an agreed upon design before putting time in the implementation.

AI assistance is very welcome! Please ensure you bring significant added value beyond the tool's output.
This is to:
- ensure you have the necessary rights to submit your contribution under the project's MIT and Apache-2.0 licenses
- respect maintainers' time: they aim to understand all code being merged as thoroughly as possible.
  The time wasted to understand "slop" is time stolen from better things.

As a rule of thumb, if reviewing your contribution takes more effort than you put into creating and submitting it,
something is deeply unbalanced, and you should refrain from submitting it.

Disclosure of AI usage is not mandatory but very much welcome!
